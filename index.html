AI English summary

Each distinct indicator contributes once. Content and URL heuristic contributions are capped at 70. Reputation contributes the strongest source signal once, capped at 90. The combined score is capped at 100. Severity bands: 0–30 Low risk, 31–69 Suspicious, 70–100 Dangerous. A low score is not a safety guarantee. Integer scores are calculated from evidence, not generated randomly by AI. A score is withheld if no applicable local check completed and no reputation result is available.

Signals include disguised executable (+40), Office macro component (+25), embedded object (+15), displayed-link mismatch (+30), brand impersonation pattern (+25), URL userinfo (+15), PDF launch action (+30), PDF JavaScript marker (+20), HTML password field with external submission (+25). Presence is an indicator, not proof of malicious intent. Repeated copies of a signal do not increase its weight.
